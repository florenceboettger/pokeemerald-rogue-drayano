# Pokémon Emerald Rogue Rebalanced

Pokémon Emerald Rogue Rebalanced is an alternate take on [Pokémon Emerald Rogue](https://github.com/Pokabbie/pokeemerald-rogue) by Pokabbie, including [further fixes and updates](https://github.com/DepressoMocha/emerogue) by DepressoMocha. All of this is based off [RHH's pokeemerald-expansion 1.7.3](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/), and made possible by the [pokeemerald decompilation project](https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald) by pret.

It aims to create a new and remixed experience by changing movesets, base stats, abilities, and move properties to give undertuned Pokémon more options and viability, occasionally nerf overtuned Pokémon or moves to bring them more in line with the main balance, and make some moves and abilities with narrow distributions more available, particularly to otherwise undertuned Pokémon. Nerfs are generally extremely limited and only applied to moves that stand too tall over other options for most Pokémon (i.e. Scald). While it's both impossible and not advisable to flatten the power levels to a completely even plane, this project aims to give some reason to use even the most usually useless Pokémon.

These changes are heavily based on the work of [Drayano](https://xcancel.com/drayano60) and his projects such as Renegade Platinum or Blaze Black 2/Volt White 2 Redux (also developed by [AphexCubed](https://x.com/aphexcubed)), with some influences by other rebalanced projects such as [Radical Red](https://www.pokecommunity.com/threads/pok%C3%A9mon-radical-red-version-4-1-released-gen-9-dlc-pokemon-character-customization-now-available.437688/). Initially, this was only seen as a way to port some of the changes from Drayano projects to Emerald Rogue (hence the project name). However, as the years of work on this project have gone on and new abilities, moves, and Pokémon have been added, this project has gained much more of our own original influences.
